# Acknowledgements & third-party components

This repository's original code is under the [MIT License](LICENSE). It does not relicense its dependencies or upstream assets.

- **[LIBERO](https://github.com/Lifelong-Robot-Learning/LIBERO)** — task definitions, environments, initial states and assets. The tested revision is recorded in the examples. Source/assets are downloaded separately and remain subject to their own licenses.
- **[robosuite](https://github.com/ARISE-Initiative/robosuite)** and **[MuJoCo](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco)** — robot control and simulation.
- **[python-fcl](https://github.com/BerkeleyAutomation/python-fcl)** / **[FCL](https://github.com/flexible-collision-library/fcl)** — independent collision-shape distance queries.
- **[Jev via OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/typesafe/jev-1.13)** — hosted decision model; access is governed by the provider's terms and pricing.
- **[Typesafe Mario](https://github.com/fhshaik/typesafe-mario)** — design inspiration for grounding a compact decision interface in measured physics. No Mario source or game assets are bundled here.

The featured GIFs/MP4s are renders of recorded LIBERO simulation episodes. They are not physical-robot footage. Upstream raw meshes, datasets, pretrained models and third-party repository copies are intentionally not vendored.
